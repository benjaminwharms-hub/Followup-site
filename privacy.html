Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 25, 2026

Harms Follow-Up Services ("we", "us") sends automated follow-up emails on behalf of small businesses ("clients"), such as construction and home-service companies, to those businesses' own customers. This policy explains what information we use, why, and the choices available to you.

1. Information from Google accounts

A client connects their Gmail account by signing in with Google and granting permission. We never see or store the client's Google password. With that permission, our application uses:

  • Send email on the client's behalf (gmail.send): to send the client's follow-up emails to the client's customers from the client's own address.
  • Read email (gmail.readonly): only to (a) confirm which Gmail address is connected, and (b) search for messages sent by the client's own customers, so that we can tell whether a customer has replied or asked to unsubscribe and stop their emails. For those messages, the application looks only at the sender, date, subject and the short preview Gmail provides.

We do not read, copy or store the contents of the client's other emails. We do not use Gmail data for advertising, we do not sell it, we do not use it to train artificial-intelligence or machine-learning models, and we do not transfer it to anyone, except as described in this policy or as required by law.

Harms Follow-Up Services' use and transfer of information received from Google APIs to any other app will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

2. Other information we handle

  • Client details: business name, sender name, email address, phone number, website, mailing address, email signature and Google review link, provided by the client.
  • Customer and job details provided by the client: customer names, email addresses, job type, job address, completion date and job status.
  • Records of emails sent: which follow-up email was sent to which customer, and when, plus a list of customers who asked not to be emailed again. These records keep anyone from being emailed twice or after opting out.
  • Access keys: the key Google provides when a client grants permission. It's stored securely and used only to operate the service for that client.

3. How we use information

Only to provide the follow-up email service to our clients: sending each customer the follow-up emails their service provider has chosen, stopping emails when a customer replies or opts out, and keeping records so the service works correctly. We do not use it for any other purpose.

4. Where information is stored and how it is protected

Information is stored on a password-protected, encrypted computer operated by Harms Follow-Up Services, and is not uploaded to third-party storage services. Emails are sent through Google's Gmail service. Access keys are kept separate from other files and are never shared.

5. Sharing

We do not sell, rent or trade personal information. Information is shared only with Google, as needed to send emails through the client's Gmail account, or when required by law.

6. How long we keep information

We keep a client's information while they use the service. When a client stops using the service, we delete their information, including their access key, within 30 days of their request. The exception is any record we must keep by law, or to honour opt-out requests.

7. Your choices

  • Clients can disconnect our application at any time from their Google Account at myaccount.google.com/permissions, or ask us to delete their data.
  • Customers who receive follow-up emails can reply "STOP" (or use the unsubscribe option in their email app) and will not be emailed again by that business. You can also contact us, or the business directly, to ask about or delete your information.

8. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update the effective date above, and tell our clients about any significant change.
