Follow-up emails your customers will actually read

We help construction and home-service businesses stay in touch with customers after every job: a thank-you, a check-in, a review request, and a seasonal reminder. The emails are sent automatically from the business's own Gmail, so they're personal and they come from you.

How it works

  1. 1. Connect your Gmail once. You sign in with Google and click Allow. We never see or store your password.
  2. 2. Mark a job complete. That's the only thing you do.
  3. 3. Your customer hears from you. Day 1: thank you. Day 7: checking everything is right. Day 14: a Google review request. Day 90: seasonal maintenance and referrals.
  4. 4. Replies come straight to you. If a customer replies, the automated emails stop so you can take it from there. Anyone who replies STOP is never emailed again.

Built to be respectful

  • Each email is a one-to-one message about a job you did. It's not a mass mailing.
  • Nobody receives the same email twice.
  • Every email includes an easy way to opt out.
  • You can disconnect at any time from your Google Account.
